# Ashes Below — Checkpoint Cinematic Script

## Narrative Frame

*Ashes Below* begins beneath a permanent-looking violet eclipse over Nocturne. The city’s emergency rail network has become an organism-like relay for an unknown signal. The player enters as a bloodline operative who can move through the sealed maintenance district without triggering the surface quarantine.

The story layer uses brief, skippable local animated overlays rather than prerecorded video. Each scene fades and rises into the mission, pauses hostile pressure, presents a timestamped archive event, and ends with a single action that returns the player to the next objective. The mobile campaign archive exposes the same chronology before a player starts or resumes the mission.

| Trigger | Timeline event | Dramatic purpose | Gameplay consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mission start | **Eclipse − 72 Minutes: The City Looked Up Too Late** | Establishes the artificial eclipse, abandoned rail grid, and the first relay as the player’s lead. | Begins at Signal Platform with three relay targets. |
| Maintenance Spine checkpoint | **Eclipse + 13 Minutes: The Maintenance Spine Became a Nerve** | Reveals that the signal travels through collective memory and explains the multiplying Hollowed. | Sends the player to Furnace Lift and introduces the Blood Wraith as an instructional carrier. |
| Furnace Lift checkpoint | **Eclipse + 24 Minutes: The Engine Learned Our Names** | Explains that the Conductor has absorbed civic emergency protocols and can anticipate containment. | Opens the Engine Core objective and frames the rail-control mechanic in the boss encounter. |

## Script Excerpts

> **Duskstalker, Event 01:** “Find the first relay. If it is listening, it can be made to speak.”

> **Archivist Ora, Event 02:** “The Wraith is not guarding the lift. It is carrying an instruction set.”

> **Archivist Ora, Event 03:** “Break its rail controls. Do not let it finish the final timetable.”

## Presentation Rules

The overlays do not claim voice acting, motion capture, or pre-rendered cinematics. Their animation is limited to original interface motion—an eclipse ring, a timeline axis, and a fade-and-rise text card. They are interruptible with **Continue to Objective**, do not request network data, and do not gate a local saved checkpoint behind payment, an ad view, or an online service.
