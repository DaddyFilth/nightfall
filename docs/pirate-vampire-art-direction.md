# NIGHTFALL: BLOOD HUNT — Blood & Brass Art Direction

NIGHTFALL now draws from a **late-1500s gothic-steampunk pirate world** where an eclipsed sea feeds clockwork cathedrals, black-sailed vessels, and aristocratic vampire crews. The player character is the **Bloodwake Captain**, an original pirate-vampire hunter whose weathered naval finery, brass mechanisms, crimson eyes, and battered tricorn form the visual anchor across the mobile and Godot experiences.

| Visual system | Direction | Use across the project |
|---|---|---|
| Shadow and iron | Charcoal-black hulls, soot, oxidized iron, deep brown-black shadows | App backgrounds, Godot stonework, card structure, enemy silhouettes |
| Blood and wine | Dried crimson, oxblood leather, dark claret glow | Combat danger, enemy eyes, Conductor attacks, primary emphasis |
| Aged brass | Tarnished gold, copper rivets, astrolabe mechanisms | Buttons, progress borders, levers, boss vitality readouts, navigation accents |
| Salt and bone | Parchment, ivory, sea fog, bleached wood | Readable body copy, nautical charts, route labels, cosmetic surfaces |
| Eclipsed sea | Desaturated teal-green moonlight and storm vapor | Secondary feedback, accessible contrast markers, harbor atmosphere |

The mobile interface uses black iron panels with thin aged-brass keylines, parchment-toned text, engraved labels, and a navigational-chart hierarchy. The Godot slice changes the Nocturne rail vocabulary into the **Brasswake Dockyards**: rotting wharf timbers, brass-pressure pipes, ship ribs, naval capstans, bell towers, and a clockwork eclipse mechanism. Enemy presentation becomes drowned deckhands and vampiric privateers with torn coats, coral-and-brass ornaments, and crimson eye-glow.

> The project uses original imagery and original names only. The theme does not introduce real-world faction symbols, paid cosmetics, gameplay bonuses, ads, account services, or remote telemetry.
