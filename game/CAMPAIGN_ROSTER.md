# Blood & Brass Native FPS Campaign Roster

The native campaign is structured as **ten sequential, animated first-person combat levels**. Each level has its own objective cadence, hostile encounter space, and **three to six checkpoint objectives**. A checkpoint is an in-level recovery and progression beat only; it never unlocks a later level. Completing a level remains the sole way to unlock the next level.

| Level | Title | Checkpoints | Interactive FPS objective rhythm |
|---:|---|---:|---|
| 01 | Ashes Below | 3 | Breach the dockyard, move the floodgate line, and destroy the first signal pyre. |
| 02 | The Broken Compass | 4 | Fight through a harbor split, resolve the crew route, cross the crane walk, and break the relay. |
| 03 | The Observatory | 4 | Enter by the saved branch, clear the aperture hall, hold the defender ring, and defeat the Drowned Admiral. |
| 04 | Sable Wake | 5 | Pursue the blackwater convoy through pier, cargo, furnace, harpoon, and wakebreaker encounters. |
| 05 | Lanterns of the Lost | 3 | Track ghostlights through the quay and maze before confronting the beacon keeper. |
| 06 | Iron Cathedral | 6 | Assault the foundry, gallery, bellworks, reliquary, guard line, and Iron Saint finale. |
| 07 | Coffin Fleet | 4 | Board the moonless armada across hull, line, crypt, and fleet-command encounters. |
| 08 | The Thirteenth Bell | 5 | Advance through the tide clock, reef, bell chamber, nave, and final strike. |
| 09 | Red Meridian | 4 | Cross the scarlet sound, bridge, vein engine, and sunless horizon. |
| 10 | Blood & Brass | 6 | Close the campaign at Crownless Harbor, the leviathan, the Admiral’s wake, eclipse deck, oath, and final tide. |

Every checkpoint is intended for moving, aiming, firing, ADS, reloading, dodging, animated enemy attacks, and a clear local completion trigger. Story unlocks are device-local and must be completed in numerical order.
