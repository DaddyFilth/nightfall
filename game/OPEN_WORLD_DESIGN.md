# Brasswake Open World

Blood & Brass uses an **open-district exploration** structure rather than an unrestricted city simulation. The player can freely traverse five connected areas of Brasswake in first person, find optional discovery beacons, fight ambient threats, and return to the Drowned Chart at any time.

| District | Exploration character | Story boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Brasswake Dockyards | Starting berth, freight yards, and galleon routes | Open from the first launch. |
| Sable Quarter | Warehouses, compass tower, and hidden cargo lanes | Optional discovery only. |
| Cathedral Causeway | Bell-road, nave approaches, and brass gates | Optional discovery only. |
| Cinder Locks | Steam canals and ember-pump platforms | Optional discovery only. |
| Iron Foreshore | Breakwater, armored batteries, and eclipse sea overlook | Optional discovery only. |

The ten campaign missions remain first-to-last. Open-world discovery records are stored locally and never unlock, skip, or alter a story mission. This preserves authored boss encounters and checkpoint gates while still giving the player a free-roam space for class practice and environmental discovery.

The world art uses only the original Blood & Brass visual direction: wet timber, oxidized brass, dark sea stone, steam infrastructure, fog, crimson lanterns, and vampire-pirate maritime architecture. The supplied application icon is not used in any world graphic or native scene.
