# Blood & Brass Campaign Encounter Contracts

Each native level resolves through a named original boss and an animated traversal set piece. Traversal objects are activated as players secure local checkpoints, and they remain part of the first-person combat space rather than replacing it with a passive scene.

| Levels | Traversal set piece | Boss | Late-game enemy roles |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01–03 | Floodgate Lift, Compass Crane, Aperture Bridge | Drowned Admiral, Compass Warden, Observatory Conductor | Privateer and Harpoon Raider establish close- and mid-range threat readability. |
| 04–05 | Wakebreaker Chain, Ghostlight Causeway | Wakebreaker Harpooner, Lantern Keeper | Harpoon Raider and Lantern Wisp add long-reach pressure and fast flanking. |
| 06–07 | Reliquary Lift, Boarding Chain | Iron Saint, Coffin Fleet Captain | Iron Abbot and Coffin Marine add armored and high-vitality boarder pressure. |
| 08–10 | Tide Clock Span, Meridian Bridge, Leviathan Spine | Bell Ringer, Meridian Blood-Engine, Brass Leviathan | Bell Tollkeeper, Meridian Sentinel, and Leviathan Guard establish fast, ranged, and heavy final-arc threats. |

All identities, attack names, vitality values, and base damage are local authored campaign data. The encounter system remains offline and does not grant gameplay-affecting monetized rewards.

## Environmental checkpoint gates

Each checkpoint is preceded by an original three-object environmental gate. Players **observe the prompt in-world, then shoot the color-marked objects in the indicated order**. A wrong object resets that short sequence, while a correct sequence opens the checkpoint beacon and enables the traversal set piece. The patterns are local authored puzzle data; they are not timing traps, paid skips, or hidden random rolls.
